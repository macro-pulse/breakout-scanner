Methodology

  • Universe: Justin Banks AI watchlist (post, graphic, ETFs) + S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100 + S&P 400. Non-watchlist names need price > $5 and 50-day average dollar volume > $20M. Screens via Finviz Elite; daily OHLCV from Yahoo Finance.
  • Tight range: 20-day high-low range ≤ max(12%, 3.5 × ATR14%) and either Bollinger Band width in the bottom 25% of its 1-year history or ATR(14)% down 15%+ vs 20 days ago. Also tracked: NR7, inside days, volume dry-up.
  • 8/21 EMA: 8-day vs 21-day EMA; "fresh" = crossed within the last 5 sessions.
  • Higher lows: each of the last 3 weekly lows above the prior one.
  • 10/40-week MA: close above the 10- and 40-week simple moving averages.
  • Breakout: close above the prior 20-day high on volume ≥ 1.5× 50-day average. Retest: broke a 20-day high in the last 10 sessions, pulled back within 3% of it, and still closes above.
  • RVOL: day volume / prior 50-day (and 20-day) average; 3-day average RVOL for accumulation. ≥ 1.5 on an up day closing in the top third = breakout volume; ≥ 2 = strong; 3-day < 0.7 = dry-up in the base.
  • Trigger: prior 20-day high (next resistance if already above). Stop: 10-day low or base low.
  • Value score (0–100): forward P/E vs sector median, PEG, P/S, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, analyst target upside, % off 52-week high.
  • Setup score (0–100): EMA state, higher lows, MAs, tight range, dry-up, NR7/inside, breakout, retest, RVOL, distance to trigger.
  • Combined = 0.4 × value + 0.6 × setup.
  • Risk %: distance from trigger down to stop, as a percent of the trigger price.
